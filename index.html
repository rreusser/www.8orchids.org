Welcome Home

Nestled in the heart of Downtown Oakland at the vibrant confluence of Old Oakland, Chinatown, and Jack London Square, 8 Orchids offers a unique opportunity to be part of a dynamic urban community. Here, historic architecture meets modern living, and diverse cultures blend to create one of the Bay Area's most exciting neighborhoods.

Just steps from BART, our location makes it effortless whether your heart is in Oakland's thriving arts scene, local eateries, and waterfront—or your workday takes you across the bay to San Francisco. This is more than a place to live; it's a place to belong.
